Why it matters

Get to know your people, all over again.

Life gets busy, and screens fill the quiet moments. Quality Questions helps you go past "how was your day?" and really hear each other, whether you've been together for one year or thirty.

Stay curious about each other
Questions for couples that go deeper than schedules and errands, so you keep discovering the person you love.
Hear what your kids are thinking
Questions that get more than a one-word answer, from little kids in car seats to teenagers in the front seat.
Make memories with friends
Swap stories, laugh a lot, and learn things you never knew about people you've known for years.
BUILT FOR THE CAR

Turn a quiet drive into a real conversation.

Quality Questions works right on your car's screen with CarPlay and Android Auto. Pick a category, tap play, and keep your eyes on the road. It's the easiest way to make the most of school pickups, errands, and long road trips.

Steering wheel buttons
Play asks a new question. Back repeats it. Next skips ahead.
Plays nicely with your music
Your music or podcast dips while the question is read, then picks right back up.
Ask with your voice
Say "Play Couples on Quality Questions" and keep both hands on the wheel.
